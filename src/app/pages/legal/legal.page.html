What we collect

  • Account: email address, username, display name, avatar settings or photo, optional phone number and clan, and your PayPal payout email if you sell land.
  • Your activity: land you claim and what you put on it, listings, connections, reports, and private messages between verified users.
  • Location: where your avatar is on the map. To start you near home we ask your browser for your location, or estimate your city from your IP address using a lookup service (get.geojs.io / ipapi.co). We do not store your precise real-world location on our servers; your avatar's map position is shared live with people nearby while you are online.
  • Nearby chat is sent live to people near you and kept only on their and your devices.
  • Payments: handled by PayPal. We receive subscription, payment and payout IDs and amounts, never your card or bank details.
  • Technical: IP address and basic logs for security and to prevent abuse.
  • On your device: we store your sign-in token, guest avatar and draft land, pins and settings in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
